    About

    The Abstract American is an evolving gallery of vibrant PNG artworks with a sci-fi/steampunk pulse. It’s meant to feel alive — neon edges, metallic reflections, and a gentle holographic shimmer. Scroll, click, and talk about what you feel. Conversation over commerce.

    The site is intentionally light and responsive, designed to showcase art crisply on phones, tablets, and desktops. New pieces are added regularly — keep an eye on the carousel.
