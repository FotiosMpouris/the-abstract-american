    About

    Hello from planet Earth. I paint with color first and logic second — mixing the familiar with the almost-impossible. Some of it feels sci-fi, some of it smells like salt air and street noise. All of it chases a feeling: that small click when a memory meets a new idea.

    If a piece makes you laugh, stare, or argue with your friend for a minute, we’re doing this right. I’m open to commissions when we share an appetite for expressive work and a healthy risk tolerance.

    More of my non-gallery projects live at fotiosmpouris.com.
