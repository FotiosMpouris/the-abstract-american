    About

    Hi — I’m a human from planet Earth making images that mix the familiar with the slightly impossible. Think city light, rust, coastline, circuitry — sometimes sci-fi, sometimes not — always guided by color, texture, and play. The “Abstract American” tag is a lens, not a rulebook.

    This site aims to feel alive: subtle neon edges, metallic whispers, a touch of hologram. The goal isn’t selling; it’s starting conversations. If a piece nudges a memory or a question, that’s the win. Thanks for spending a moment here.
