    About

    An artist and dedicated builder driven by the absolute joy of creation, development, and animation; with countless hours spent crafting art and technology. My ultimate creative mission is to produce art and code. I have spent countless hours learning, creating and building digital art; diving pretty deep into the Adobe Creative Suite, Blender, 3dsMax and also utilizing Unity for simulated games and applications. I have also spent a lot of time learning to employ Python’s magic into sophisticated Agentic Applications. Whether I am designing a captivating 3D experience or creating custom ai tools, my goal is always to craft solutions that solve real problems; my mind attemting to find solutions and curate clarity from the throes of complexity. I find great joy in building and creating, whether art, custom tools, web design, automated workflows; my brain just feasts on all of it. My motivations lie in building projects where art, tech, and heart converge. If you are launching a bold idea, or crafting something that challenges the ordinary—something entertaining, educational, or deeply meaningful—I am here to build it with you, step by inspired step.

    A lot of pieces in this collection are studies. There will be more. This is only the beginning.
